Privacy Policy

Summary

This app does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. It has no account, no advertising, no analytics, no in-app purchases and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (scrap, upgrade tokens, the cars you own and their upgrades, starter missions, your race settings and your ghost-mode best runs) and your settings are stored only in the app's storage on your device so you can continue where you left off. We cannot see or access this data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes everything.

If Android's own backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's data in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is managed by Android and your Google account; we have no access to it.

Permissions

  • Network access (INTERNET): declared by the app framework. The app itself makes no network requests.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
